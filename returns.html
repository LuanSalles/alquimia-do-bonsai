Returns and exchanges

Each bonsai is a living and unique piece. Before purchase, we recommend confirming updated photos, measurements, availability, shipping conditions and care needs through the official contact channel.

Living products

Because bonsai and pre-bonsai are living products, returns and exchanges are evaluated case by case. Plant stress caused by incorrect watering, lack of light, unsuitable environment or delayed care after delivery may not qualify for return.

Problems on arrival

If a living product arrives damaged, contact us as soon as possible with photos and order details so we can evaluate the best solution.

Digital items

Digital books, PDFs and educational materials are generally considered delivered once access is provided. If there is a problem with the link or file access, contact us and we will help.

Português

Cada bonsai é uma peça viva e única. Antes da compra, recomendamos confirmar fotos atualizadas, medidas, disponibilidade, envio e cuidados pelo canal oficial.

Por serem produtos vivos, trocas e devoluções são avaliadas caso a caso. Danos causados por rega incorreta, falta de luz, ambiente inadequado ou ausência de cuidado após a entrega podem não se enquadrar como troca.
