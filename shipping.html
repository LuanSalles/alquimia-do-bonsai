Shipping Policy · Politica de Envio

Shipping and delivery

Bonsai Alchemy / Alquimia do Bonsai works with living trees, unique catalog pieces, books and educational materials. Shipping conditions may vary according to the product, destination, season, weather and plant health.

Living bonsai and pre-bonsai

Before confirming an order, we check the current availability of the tree, packaging needs, destination and shipping viability. Some living pieces may require local pickup or special delivery arrangements.

Books and digital materials

Digital materials are delivered through official links or direct contact. Physical books may be purchased through Amazon or another channel indicated on the website.

Brazil and international orders

Brazilian catalog inquiries are handled through the Portuguese contact channel. International and United States inquiries are handled through the English contact channel. Delivery options and final costs are confirmed before payment.

Português

A Alquimia do Bonsai trabalha com árvores vivas, peças únicas, livros e materiais educativos. As condições de envio podem variar conforme produto, destino, clima, época do ano e saúde da planta.

Antes de confirmar o pedido, verificamos disponibilidade atual, embalagem, destino e viabilidade do envio. Algumas peças podem exigir retirada local ou entrega especial.
